Purpose

The only thing between page load and play. Every element not shown here is deliberately absent: no menu, no settings, no account, no "how to play", no scroll.

Why it exists at all

It costs us part of the cold-start advantage that is our main edge over every competitor (JRN-001 STEP-001/002). It earns its place by doing the job the absent tutorial would otherwise do: the labels let the player self-declare experience rather than decode abstract tiers, which is the onboarding pattern the research endorses.

Requirements

  • FR-001 / FR-002 — gate shown on load; selection starts the match immediately.
  • FR-026 / EDGE-002 — fully keyboard operable, with a visible focus ring (shown above as the orange outline).
  • Time budget: this screen plus the first meaningful action must fit inside 30 s total.

States

Default · Focused · Loading (first render < 3 s) · WebGL unavailable — EDGE-001 replaces the canvas with a plain readable message, never a blank rectangle.
