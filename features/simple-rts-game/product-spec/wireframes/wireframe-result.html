Change difficulty

Purpose

End the match unambiguously and get the player back in. For a bounded game the rematch button is the retention loop — we deliberately forgo the multiplayer retention mechanism every competitor leads with (RF-5), so this screen carries disproportionate weight.

Requirements

  • FR-019 — Rematch is the primary and largest action. Nothing competes with it for prominence.
  • JRN-002 STEP-002 — Rematch restarts at the same difficulty without returning to the gate. "Change difficulty" is deliberately small and secondary.
  • EDGE-001 — the defeat variant is identical in structure: no penalty, no extra step, no post-mortem. A player who just lost is the one most likely to leave.

Match stats

Duration is shown because it is a success criterion the player can feel (6–10 min target). The other two figures are flavour and feed the local counters in metrics.md — no analytics provider, no backend.

States

Victory (shown) · Defeat · Draw — identical layout in all three; verdict text and colour differ. Verdict must not be conveyed by colour alone; the word carries the meaning. Draw occurs when both Bases reach zero HP on the same tick (FR-028, JRN-001 EDGE-009) — rare, but a real branch, so it is specified rather than resolved by an arbitrary tie-break.
